ClinicFlow · Privacy

Privacy Policy

This policy explains what information ClinicFlow uses to help people request and manage clinic appointments through Messenger.

Last updated: October 5, 2026

In brief: ClinicFlow processes information needed to handle an appointment conversation, coordinate scheduling, and keep related appointment records. It is an appointment-coordination tool. It does not diagnose, give medical advice, or store full medical records.

Information ClinicFlow processes

  • Messenger conversation information: Meta/Messenger identifiers and message content needed to identify the conversation, understand a request, and respond or route it.
  • Appointment details: patient name and contact number, requested service, appointment date and time, and booking, rescheduling, or cancellation state.
  • Scheduling and operational records: Google Calendar event data, local ClinicFlow operational appointment records, and Google Sheets appointment records.

Please send only information needed to arrange an appointment. Do not send detailed medical histories or other sensitive health information through the conversation.

How information is used

ClinicFlow uses this information to interpret appointment requests, ask for or confirm appointment details, check and coordinate availability, carry out booking, rescheduling, or cancellation requests, maintain related operational records, and route requests that need attention to clinic staff. Automated booking actions are verified against the scheduling provider. Some requests may be escalated to clinic staff.

Where applicable, OpenAI may process conversation content to support AI interpretation of a request. ClinicFlow does not use this process to diagnose patients or provide medical advice.

Services involved

Depending on the participating clinic’s setup, information may be processed by:

  • Meta / Messenger to carry the conversation between you and the clinic’s Messenger presence.
  • Google Calendar for appointment availability and event information, and Google Sheets for appointment records.
  • n8n to connect and automate ClinicFlow workflow steps.
  • OpenAI where used for AI interpretation of conversation content.

These providers process information as needed for the ClinicFlow service and are also subject to their own terms and privacy practices.

Clinic and staff access

Appointment information may be available to the participating clinic and its authorized staff so they can manage scheduling and respond to requests. The clinic or provider is responsible for its clinical services and its own records. ClinicFlow does not replace clinic staff, verify HMO eligibility, or process card payments.

Retention and your choices

This policy does not set a fixed retention period. Some information may need to be retained for legitimate operational, security, or legal reasons. You can stop sending messages at any time and may request access to, correction of, or deletion of ClinicFlow information, subject to applicable law and records that need to be retained for those reasons.

To make a privacy request, email maisoglabsclinicflow@gmail.com. Include enough information to help locate the conversation or appointment, such as the name used and an approximate appointment date. Do not send passwords or unnecessary health information. We may ask a follow-up question to identify the relevant record. See the data deletion instructions.

Updates and contact

This policy may be updated if ClinicFlow’s operation changes. The latest update date appears at the top. For privacy questions or requests, contact maisoglabsclinicflow@gmail.com.
