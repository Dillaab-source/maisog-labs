Data Deletion Instructions

You can ask us to delete information associated with your ClinicFlow conversation or appointment.

Last updated: October 5, 2026

To request deletion: Email maisoglabsclinicflow@gmail.com with the subject “ClinicFlow data deletion request.”

What to include

  1. Say that you want your ClinicFlow information deleted.
  2. Include enough detail to help us find the right record, such as the name used for the appointment, an approximate appointment date, and the clinic or Messenger conversation involved if known.
  3. Do not include passwords, payment details, or unnecessary health information. If we cannot confidently identify the record, we may contact you to clarify before acting.

What happens to the request

We will review the request and identify the ClinicFlow information involved. Where we can identify the information and deletion is appropriate, we will arrange deletion from the relevant ClinicFlow operational records and appointment records, which may include records held in Google Calendar or Google Sheets, with the participating clinic as needed.

Some information may need to be retained for legitimate operational, security, or legal reasons. If that applies, we will limit retention to what is needed for that reason. This page does not promise a fixed retention period.

Other services

This request is for information processed through ClinicFlow. It does not delete messages from your Messenger account or records independently held by Meta, Google, or the clinic. You can also use the relevant provider’s own privacy tools or contact the clinic about its records.

Contact

Questions about a deletion request? Email maisoglabsclinicflow@gmail.com. For more information about what ClinicFlow processes, read the Privacy Policy.
