Last updated: October 5, 2026

What ClinicFlow does

ClinicFlow helps participating clinics handle appointment requests through Messenger and coordinate appointment details with the clinic’s scheduling provider. It may use automated tools to interpret a request. Automated booking actions are verified against the scheduling provider, and some requests may be escalated to clinic staff.

Acceptable use

Use ClinicFlow only for lawful appointment coordination. Provide information you are authorized to share and keep it accurate. Do not impersonate someone, submit another person’s information without permission, interfere with the service, attempt unauthorized access, or send abusive, unlawful, or harmful content. Send only what is needed to arrange an appointment; do not send detailed medical histories or emergency requests through ClinicFlow.

Appointments and clinical care

ClinicFlow does not diagnose, treat, or give medical advice. For medical questions, contact a qualified healthcare professional. For an emergency, contact local emergency services or seek urgent care rather than relying on Messenger or ClinicFlow.

The participating clinic or provider is responsible for its clinical care, appointment availability, schedule, and services. ClinicFlow does not replace clinic staff, verify HMO eligibility, process card payments, or store full medical records.

Bookings, changes, and cancellations

A request is not a confirmed appointment until the scheduling provider reflects the booking or clinic staff confirms it. You may ask to reschedule or cancel, but the change is effective only after it is verified against the scheduling provider or confirmed by clinic staff. Follow any instructions or notice requirements provided by the clinic. If you do not receive confirmation, contact the clinic directly.

Availability and third-party services

ClinicFlow is a pilot and may be delayed, unavailable, or changed or discontinued. It depends on services such as Meta Messenger, Google Calendar, Google Sheets, n8n, and OpenAI where used.
