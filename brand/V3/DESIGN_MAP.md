# MAISOG LABS — V3.1 APPROVED DESIGN MAP

## Source of truth
Use the approved V3.1 visual reference supplied by Paulo as the primary visual reference. Do not replace its composition with a generic space background, generic stock architecture, or a CSS-only approximation.

## Background composition
- LEFT 0–48%: deep blue-black space and the illuminated curved Earth horizon.
- CENTER 45–67%: transition zone, dark negative space, bright star/light source near the upper transition.
- RIGHT 55–100%: monumental Roman/Greek classical arch environment with warm stone.
- FAR RIGHT / THROUGH ARCH: bright mountain valley / atmospheric landscape.
- COOL/WARM SPLIT: cool space/earth on the left; warm daylight architecture on the right.
- The Earth and architecture must visually belong to one continuous cinematic scene.

## Header map
- Top-left: small canonical Maisog Labs orbital mark.
- Center/top: navigation.
- Top-right: restrained rounded contact CTA.
- Header should stay thin and subordinate to the hero.

## Hero map
- Center: large canonical MAISOG LABS orbital wordmark.
- Directly below: `Ideas into systems.`
- Below that: small practical-technology descriptor.
- CTA row under copy.
- Keep enough negative space around the logo; do not cover it with floating cards.

## Floating-system map
- Far-left vertical microcopy: EXPLORE / AUTOMATE / LEARN / BUILD / GROW.
- Lower-left: CURRENT FOCUS glass panel.
- Lower-right: SYSTEM STATUS glass panel.
- Additional small utility metadata may sit near the far-right edge.
- Glass UI must look functional, not decorative.

## Project system

1. ClinicFlow — workflow / booking / n8n visual.

## Non-negotiables

- No random sci-fi stock art.
